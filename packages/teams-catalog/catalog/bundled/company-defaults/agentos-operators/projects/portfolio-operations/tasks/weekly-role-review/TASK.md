Review a representative sample of completed work and corrections for the installed role version. Compare acceptance outcomes, rework, failures and cost using native task/run evidence. Propose one bounded improvement when justified. Record package content hash, sampled work references, expected benefit, evaluation procedure and rollback target.

Keep private project knowledge out of shared instructions. Ask an independent reviewer to evaluate the proposed version on equivalent tasks before promotion. Do not silently replace roles, permissions, credentials or historical evidence. If there is too little evidence, record that finding and finish without manufacturing a change.
